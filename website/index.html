FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is VitalUp free?

Yes. Every tracker, trend, report, workout, widget and the Arena are free. AI features have a daily allowance, and VitalUp Pro raises it.

Which phones can use it?

VitalUp is on Android 8.0 and newer, through Google Play. An iPhone version isn't available yet.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Logging, trends, timers and GPS workouts all work offline, and you can download maps for the areas you run or ride. AI features and the Arena need a connection. Your logs back up when you're online again.

Does it work with my watch?

If your watch or fitness app saves to Health Connect, VitalUp can read your steps, sleep, workouts, weight and heart data from there. You can also pair a Bluetooth heart rate strap for workouts.

What does Vita do with my data?

Vita only sees your data after you agree, and only to answer you. Your name and email are not sent. Health Connect readings stay out unless you switch them on. Chats are kept on your phone, not on our servers.

Read more in the privacy policy.

Is VitalUp a medical device?

No. VitalUp is for general wellness. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor before big changes to your diet or exercise.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile, then Account details, then Delete account. You can also ask us on the web.

How do I get help?

Ask Vita or open Help and support in the app, or email support@vitalup.live.
