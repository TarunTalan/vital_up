Delete your account

You can permanently delete your VitalUp account and everything linked to it at any time. The fastest way is in the app. If you can't open the app, send us a request below.

Fastest, takes effect right away

Delete in the app

  1. Open VitalUp and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to your account details.
    ProfileAccount detailsDelete account
  3. Confirm. Your account, its data and your profile photo are deleted from our servers straight away, and the app's data is wiped from your phone.

Want a copy first? Use Profile > Account details > Export my data before deleting. Deleted data can't be recovered.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile and profile photo
  • Meals, water, sleep, weight, mood and screen-time settings
  • Workouts and their GPS routes
  • Health questionnaire answers and backups
  • Points, badges, friends and challenges
  • Your subscription record at RevenueCat

What stays

  • Emails about your request, so we can show it was handled
  • Products you added to the food catalogue, which aren't linked to you
  • Your Google Play subscription, until you cancel it in Google Play
