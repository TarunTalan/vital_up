Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Store Data Safety policies and GDPR/CCPA regulations, you have the right to permanently delete your VitalUp account and all personal, biological, workout, and nutritional records associated with it.

Warning: Deletion is irreversible. Once processed, all historical workout routes, step records, macro logs, Vita coach conversation history, streak badges, and biometric sync records will be permanently expunged.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

For immediate deletion without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Open the VitalUp app on your device.
  2. Tap Profile → Settings → Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm your choice. Your local database is wiped immediately and your cloud account is purged.
