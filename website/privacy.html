1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to VitalUp ("we", "us", "our", or the "Application"). VitalUp is a holistic health, fitness, and lifestyle companion designed to help you monitor physical activity, sleep hygiene, nutrition, hydration, and cardiovascular wellbeing through smart tracking, offline-first data synchronization, and personalized wellness coaching.

Your privacy and biological data integrity are our highest priority. This Privacy Policy discloses in full transparency what data we collect, why we need it, how it is processed and protected, and your rights over your data.

Core Privacy Principle: VitalUp is engineered with an offline-first architecture. Your raw data is saved locally on your device in secure sandboxed storage. We do not sell your personal data or health statistics to advertisers, brokers, or marketing networks.

2. Google Health Connect Policy Compliance Limited Use Requirement

VitalUp integrates with Google Health Connect to read and sync fitness and biometric metrics recorded by your Android device and compatible wearables.

Google Health Connect Limited Use Commitment

VitalUp's use of information received from Google Health Connect adheres strictly to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  1. Sole Purpose: Health Connect data is accessed exclusively to provide and improve user-facing health, workout, and wellness tracking features inside VitalUp (such as daily ring summaries, macro calculations, trend analytics, and workout dashboards).
  2. No Sale of Health Data: We will NEVER sell, rent, lease, or monetize Health Connect data to any third party, data broker, health insurance firm, or advertiser.
  3. No Advertising Usage: We will NEVER transfer or use Health Connect data to serve personalized, contextual, or retargeted advertisements.
  4. No Foundation Model Training: Health Connect data is NEVER used to train or refine generalized artificial intelligence foundation models.
  5. Minimal Data Access: VitalUp only requests read permissions for the specific metric types you explicitly authorize. We do not write data to Health Connect without your consent.

Health Connect Data Types Read by VitalUp:

Health Connect Permission Data Read Purpose inside VitalUp
READ_STEPS & READ_DISTANCE Daily step count, distance traveled Calculate daily movement rings, distance stats, and active streaks.
READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED & READ_EXERCISE Active energy expended, workout sessions Adjust macronutrient targets and track exercise progression.
READ_SLEEP Sleep session duration and sleep stages Calculate sleep hygiene score and recovery recommendations.
READ_HEART_RATE, READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE, READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITY Heart rate (BPM), resting BPM, HRV Monitor cardiovascular intensity during workouts and physiological stress.
READ_OXYGEN_SATURATION & READ_BLOOD_PRESSURE SpO2 levels, systolic/diastolic BP Generate user-requested health trend summaries and clinician-ready reports.
READ_WEIGHT Body weight measurements Calibrate personalized BMR and nutritional calorie budgets.

3. Information We Collect & Why

Depending on which features you use, we collect the following categories of data:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Credentials: Email address and hashed password, or Google Sign-In profile (handled via Supabase Auth).
  • Profile Metrics: Age, biological sex, height, weight, activity level, and dietary preferences (e.g., keto, balanced, high protein) provided during onboarding to calculate your personalized nutritional goals.

B. Activity, Hydration & Wellness Logs

  • Manual Logs: Water consumption logs, food intake logs, custom workout notes, and daily mood/stress check-ins.
  • Gamification Data: Earned achievements, streak counts, arena points, and friendly challenge participation.

C. Image Data (Meal Photos & Barcodes)

  • When you use the AI Food Scanner, you can take a picture of your plate or scan a food barcode.
  • The image is processed to recognize food items and compute nutritional values against USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Images are not retained for facial recognition or surveillance.

D. Technical & Crash Diagnostics

  • App version, operating system version, device model, and anonymous crash telemetry (via Firebase Crashlytics) to resolve performance bugs and errors.

5. AI Vision & Coaching Processing

VitalUp incorporates artificial intelligence to deliver meal detection and coaching assistance:

  • AI Food Vision (Google Gemini Flash & Groq Vision): When you scan a meal, the cropped food picture is transmitted over an encrypted TLS connection to our vision API endpoint to identify meal items, volume estimates, and macronutrients. These photos are processed ephemerally and discarded once recognized.
  • Vita AI Health Coach: Vita answers wellness questions based on contextual guidelines. Vita does not replace certified professional medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency healthcare services.

6. Third-Party Services & Subprocessors

We work only with reputable cloud infrastructure providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements:

  • Supabase (Authentication, PostgreSQL Database, Storage): Houses your encrypted cloud backups under Row-Level Security (RLS). Hosted in secure tier-4 data centers.
  • Google Firebase (Firebase Messaging, Crashlytics): Used to deliver push notifications and capture non-identifiable crash stacks.
  • Google Health Connect: Android platform API for health synchronization.
  • Mapbox / OpenStreetMap: Provides map tiles for route rendering during outdoor running/cycling.
  • USDA FoodData Central / Open Food Facts: Public databases for ingredient and nutrient verification.

7. Data Storage, Sync & Security

We implement layered technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between VitalUp and remote endpoints is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases utilize AES-256 encryption. Sensitive local credentials (tokens, keys) are stored in the Android Keystore via flutter_secure_storage.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): In Supabase, database tables enforce RLS policies, ensuring each authenticated user can query only their own rows.
  • Local Autonomy: An offline-first local database (Isar) allows you to log vitals and workouts even without an active internet connection.

8. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have complete ownership and control over your personal and biological data. You may exercise the following rights at any time without penalty:

How to Delete Your Data

  1. In-App Self-Service: Navigate to Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account & Data. Confirming deletion immediately wipes your local database and schedules your cloud account and all associated records for permanent deletion.
  2. Web Request: You can submit a deletion request directly at our dedicated portal:
    Open Account & Data Deletion Page →
  3. Email Request: Send an email to privacy@vitalup.live with the subject line "Delete My VitalUp Account" from your registered account email. We will process and permanently purge your records within 30 days.

Upon confirmed deletion, all your profile details, historical health sync data, workout maps, and meal logs are irrevocably erased from our primary and backup databases.

9. Children's Privacy

VitalUp is designed for adults and young adults seeking fitness and lifestyle tracking. We do not knowingly collect personal or health information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has provided us with their information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly purge the data.
