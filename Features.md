# **VitalUp**

## **Overview**

 VitalUp is a comprehensive health and wellness platform that helps users build sustainable habits through activity tracking, nutrition logging, mindful routines, and personalised coaching. It combines real-time data with manual entries, smart reminders, and an AI-powered health coach to deliver tailored insights and motivation. With features like steps, sleep, hydration, meditation, and calorie tracking integrated into a unified dashboard, users can easily monitor their overall well-being. Streaks, challenges, and community features further encourage consistency, helping users stay engaged and committed to their long-term health goals.

## **Features**

1. Authentication & Security  
* OAuth login and sign-up  
* Secure token‑based session handling  
2. Profile Creation  
* Users can create and edit profiles 

3. ## Activity & health tracking

* ## Steps tracking   Tracks the user’s daily step count, distance covered, and overall activity level.

* Heart rate tracking  
  Monitors the user’s heart rate over time and highlights trends in resting and active heart rates.  
* Sleep data  
  Records sleep duration and basic sleep patterns such as total sleep time per day.  
* Walking tracker  
  Log dedicated walking sessions with details such as duration, distance, and pace.  
* Phone screen tracking  
  Tracks daily screen usage time.  
* Calorie tracking  
  Records calories consumed per day using the food scanner.  
* Nutrition/food tracking  
  Maintains a daily log of meals, snacks, and basic nutritional information (e.g., calories).  
* Water intake tracking  
  Keeps a record of the amount of water the user drinks each day and compares it to a daily goal.  
4. Streaks & Challenges
